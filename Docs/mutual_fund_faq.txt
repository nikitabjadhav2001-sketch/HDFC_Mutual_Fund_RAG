HDFC Mutual Fund FAQ & Information Guide

1. HDFC Large Cap Fund
Expense Ratio: The Direct Plan expense ratio for HDFC Large Cap Fund is approximately 0.98%, and the Regular Plan expense ratio is approximately 1.74%.
Fund Category: Equity - Large Cap.

2. Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)
Lock-in Period: ELSS mutual fund schemes have a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. You cannot redeem or transfer your investment before 3 years from the date of purchase.

3. Capital Gains Statement Download
Procedure to download capital-gains statement:
Step 1: Log in to your mutual fund distributor platform or AMC portal (e.g., CAMS Online, KFintech, or Groww/Zerodha).
Step 2: Go to the "Reports" or "Tax Statements" section.
Step 3: Select "Capital Gains Statement" and choose the relevant Financial Year.
Step 4: Click "Generate PDF/Excel" or "Email Statement" to download your official statement.

4. Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) vs Lump Sum
- SIP Definition: A Systematic Investment Plan allows you to invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (daily, monthly, quarterly) into a mutual fund scheme.
- Lump Sum Definition: A lump sum investment is a one-time single investment of a larger sum of money.
- Minimum SIP Amount: Most mutual fund schemes allow starting a monthly SIP with as low as Rs. 100 or Rs. 500.

5. NAV (Net Asset Value)
- What is NAV: Net Asset Value represents the per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme. It is calculated daily at the close of stock market trading hours.
- NAV Cut-off Timing: For liquid funds, the cut-off time for same-day NAV is typically 1:30 PM. For equity and debt funds, the cut-off time is 3:00 PM.

6. Exit Load
- What is Exit Load: An exit load is a fee charged by the AMC when an investor redeems or switches out of a mutual fund scheme before a specified duration.
- Equity Fund Exit Load: Many standard equity schemes charge a 1% exit load if units are redeemed within 365 days of allotment. No exit load applies after 1 year.

7. Capital Gains Tax on Mutual Funds
- Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) Equity: Gains on equity fund units held for 12 months or less are taxed at 20%.
- Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) Equity: Gains on equity fund units held for more than 12 months above Rs. 1.25 Lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% without indexation.

8. KYC (Know Your Customer) Verification
- What is KYC: KYC is a mandatory regulatory requirement by SEBI to verify the identity and address of investors before investing in mutual funds.
- Documents Required for KYC: PAN card, Aadhaar card or valid Passport/Voter ID, and a passport-size photo.
